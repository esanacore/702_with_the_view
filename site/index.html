How it works

Where your ice comes from

Most refrigerators tap whatever water the wall gives them. This one is fed by a dedicated filtered cold-water line — so the filtering happens before the water ever reaches the icemaker.

  1. The building's cold line, same as any tap.

  2. An inline filter on the way to the refrigerator — not a pitcher, not a door filter.

  3. Brand new, installed with the rest of the GE suite.

  4. Ice made from filtered water, on its own, all day.

Filter model and replacement schedule are going into the property guide.
