The Anchorage

A gated community on the water

The Anchorage at Freeport is a gated waterfront community with 24-hour security, one block from the restaurants and marinas of Freeport's Nautical Mile.

  • Gated entry, 24-hour security
  • In-ground pool
  • Tennis & pickleball courts
  • Clubhouse & gym
  • Waterfront boardwalk
  • Dock space (subject to availability, extra fee)
  • Assigned parking space
  • One block to the Nautical Mile

Freeport's LIRR station is nearby — roughly 45 minutes to Penn Station.
